Overview

Handell, a single hand, wakes up to find itself detached from its arm, and sets off to find its long-lost body. Janken is a 2D turn-based RPG in the spirit of the Mother, Dragon Quest, and Undertale series, built around a combat system based on rock-paper-scissors.

The catch: you only get one chance.

The problem

The game had to fit a tight set of constraints:

  • Game logic written entirely in C++.
  • Graphics through OpenGL and the X11 protocol, audio through OpenAL.
  • Built on top of Gordon Griesel's background framework.
  • A maximum size of 5 MB.

What I built

  • Round-based combat across 20 levels, with attack order decided by rock-paper-scissors.
  • Health and damage stats for both the player and enemies.
  • An animated player model with animated backgrounds.
  • Unique sprites and dialogue for every enemy you meet.
  • Keyboard-driven GUI for every interaction.
